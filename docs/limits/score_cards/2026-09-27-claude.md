## Why each grade was earned

### Research integrity — A−
- **Every headline verdict reproduces from committed data** (Agent A, findings 1–5): Exp 60 EARNED
  (5 v 5, p = 1/252); Exp 61 EARNED (12/12 v 0/24, 0/12, 0/24; p 7.99e-10); Exp 62 rung A EARNED,
  key-for-key equal to its verdict file; R3's frozen INCOMPLETE and amended COMPLETE both reproduce;
  Exp 56 and its 1.20.4 re-baseline PASS. Each rests on one clean-tree hash that is an ancestor of the tag.
- **Prereg-before-data is enforced in CI and now reaches 1.3's experiments** (52 governed entries,
  7 grandfathered and reported as still failing, with a positive control).
- **Corrections are made in place, dated**: Exp 61's unsigned bundles, the void Exp 37/38 arm,
  Exp 60's run 1 kept as an instrument null.
- **Deciding gap:** validation beyond the frozen gate is not enforced. The Exp 10 re-run that moved a
  ledger row stamps no commit (operator-attested) and consists of typed aborts D22 calls not-data, by
  owner override; `rerun_*` data sit outside the prereg lint; POST-DATA amendments and in-place prereg
  edits are noted, not judged. Exp 56's original no-op kit cannot be re-run from committed files.
- **To reach A:** stamp commit, clean-tree and `n_ctx` in sim `report.json` and lint any ledger status
  change citing a record without them; bring `rerun_*` data under the provenance lint; make an
  in-place prereg edit after data a failure; commit Exp 56's `pair0_artifacts` or mark the kit
  non-reproducible.

### Ambition and originality — B+
