## 2. Ambition and originality — B

**VERIFIED — the system has working, distinctive integration.** The survival results compose
sensors, situation encoding, learned valence, action choice, export and ingestion. This is
production wiring: `runtime/agent_loop.py:4514` calls `propose_via_substrate`, whose fear read at
line 1634 calls NAc; the returned proposal reaches the executor. Exp 61's donor/receiver harness
uses real CLI export/ingest, and its no-op controls fail. Exp 56 additionally demonstrates a
taught want moving through the same general substrate-sharing design. The reproduced effects
earn this grade, not the biological names or roadmap.

**VERIFIED — the deciding gap is breadth.** Exp 62 transfers within a sealed-shell, frozen-day
class with one discriminating world sensor; it does not establish invariance to a changed
situation. The ledger names the time-of-day wrap limitation (#899). R3's survival endpoint is
at ceiling; faster surfacing and reduced pain are the demonstrated gains. Exp 37/38 do not
establish reliable override of LLM priors. Memory-strength recording is explicitly not a
calibrated behavioral result. Existing issues #908/#909 also describe missing cerebellar
persistence/consumption; `runtime/bio_stack.py:458` constructs a default CerebellumConfig while
its saver at lines 80–91 requires a configured path. **UNVERIFIED:** field-wide scientific
novelty, general intelligence, and broad cross-environment learning. No literature-priority
claim is awarded.

**To reach B+:** graduate an additional changed-context or multi-step task under a preregistered
held-out design, with real production consumers, mechanism ablations and committed provenance;
make removal of the claimed mechanism fail the behavioral gate.
