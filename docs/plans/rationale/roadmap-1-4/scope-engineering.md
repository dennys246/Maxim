# Roadmap 1.4 "Anticipation" — SCOPE + ENGINEERING lens (design review)

**Reviewed:** `docs/plans/roadmap_1_4.md` DRAFT v1 (2026-09-18), untracked at review time (`?? docs/plans/roadmap_1_4.md`; `docs/plans/rationale/roadmap-1-4/` existed and was empty).
**Charter:** CLAUDE.md "Working principles for new mechanisms" (front-gate scope pressure, two-tier invariants, dormancy over deletion, convergence vs divergence, regression-guard citation) + release governance (CLAUDE.md §Versioning, `docs/publication_guide.md`, the ledger lifecycle in `docs/plans/behavioral_graduation_candidates.md`).
**Reviewer:** Claude (Fable 5.1), 2026-09-18. Line numbers are against the untracked draft as read; they will drift once the file is edited — cite symbols/headings when folding.

## Verdict in one paragraph

The thesis, the ladder order, the variant-body rule and the "each mechanism enters only when a rung names it" posture are right and consistent with the repo's disciplines. The plan has three structural problems the charter exists to catch: **(1) Phase 0 builds ahead of named gaps** — three of its five instrument items (the per-*step* credit ledger, the full grid world, the learning-curve statistic) are E2/E3's instruments, yet T1 makes all five a 1.4.0 gate while E2/E3 are themselves optional (T5/T6) — a release could ship instruments whose only consumer is an experiment that did not run (the D43 "capability, not fix" family); **(2) the front-gate audit list for Phase 5 omits the two pieces of existing infrastructure most directly on point** — `embodiment/cerebellum.py` (a shipped forward model) and `decisions/temporal_credit.py::TemporalCreditDistributor` + `NAc.update_eligibility` (the shipped eligibility trace), so "a predictor enters through a NEW plan" is pre-answered before the audit; **(3) the deferral is not recorded where the plans audit reads** — the shared-perception deferral, the Phase 1b reflex-tier trigger, Phase 6 intrinsic motivation, and the JEPA re-point each leave a contradicting document behind. The ledger analysis also shows the variant-body rule shields less than the plan says: Phase 0 items 1 and 4 fire Exp 56/60/61 triggers by their letter through shared code (the bridge and `recommend_action`), and the minor-version heartbeat fires every Tier-1 row regardless of body. None of this is DO-NOT-BUILD for the roadmap as a whole; one item is DO-NOT-BUILD as scoped.

## Findings, ranked

### DO-NOT-BUILD (as scoped)

**DNB-1 — Phase 0 items 1b/3/5 build ahead of a named gap; T1 gates 1.4.0 on instruments whose consumers are optional.**
Evidence: roadmap L75–76 states the scoping rule ("each scoped by the first rung that needs it"), then L77–105 scopes items to E3's needs, and L247–249 (T1) requires all five before 1.4.0 while L257–260 (T5/T6) make E2/E3 optional. Per item:
- Item 1 (L77–86) bundles two different reads. The **per-need** breakdown of the `drive` score component is E1's need (L143: "decision provenance … which need won") and is small — `NAc.recommend_action` already iterates drives by name and records each contribution as a string in `parts` (`src/maxim/decisions/nac.py` L2159–2196: `parts.append(f"drive:{drive_name}({drive_value:.2f}) name-match")`), so the change is an additive telemetry field beside `comp["drive"]` (L2100), guarded by the existing byte-identical-selection test (`tests/unit/test_decision_provenance.py` L18). The **per-step credit** ledger ("the credit each earlier step received", L79–80) is E2/E3's need (E1 keeps R4 out "by construction", L135) and lives on a different surface entirely: the credit WRITE path (`NAc.credit_node` / `credit_operant_reward` / `record_cluster_fear` / `TemporalCreditDistributor.distribute` / `update_eligibility`), not the recommend event. Naming the recommend event as the `src/` change for both is the wrong seam for half of it — and the "real consumer" red gate (L113–114) has no named consumer for the per-step half.
- Item 3 (L93–96): "positions, a light gradient, air pockets and oxygen over a path" is E2/E3's world. E1 (L134–135: food at depth *d* in a lit column, one or two primitives from the food) needs `scripted_water.py` + depth + a food anchor. `scripted_water.py` is already modelled on `exp56.common.ScriptedBridgeServer` (anchor-following snapshots; `scripts/survival_world/scripted_water.py` L1–16) — the extension is a second anchor, not a grid.
- Item 5 (L102–104): episodes-to-criterion is E2's DV (L156: "return rate across episodes") and E3's (L176–177). E1's statistic is a depth × arm interaction (L142). Nothing in Phase 1–2 consumes it.
Why this is DNB rather than SF: the plan's own T1 makes these a release gate. If E2/E3 slip to 1.4.x (allowed by T5/T6), 1.4.0 ships a per-step credit read, a grid world and a learning-curve statistic with **zero non-test consumers** — exactly the "shipped the pieces, not the composition" shape CLAUDE.md warns about, one level up (an instrument with no experiment is the harness analogue of a function with no caller).
Recommended text (replace L72–76 opener and T1):
> **Phase 0 — the instrument for E1 (step zero).** The R3 process layer carries unchanged. E1 needs three things, each two-lens reviewed and run offline before the rig: (1a) the per-need breakdown of the `drive` component on the `NAc_RECOMMEND` event (additive field beside `comp`, byte-identical selection guarded); (2) the campaign core, extracted (§below); (4) primitive motor verbs on the bridge, live-measured. **Instrument increments owned by later rungs enter with their rung, not before:** (1b) the per-step credit ledger read from the credit write path (E2's prereg names the consumer: `credit_operant_reward` / `record_cluster_fear` / `TemporalCreditDistributor.distribute`); (3) the grid world's light gradient and air pockets (E2); (5) episodes-to-criterion (E2/E3). Each carries its own strict red gate, written when its rung's prereg is frozen.
> **T1 — E1's instrument shipped and proven:** items 1a, 2, 4 merged with their offline runs; R3 and Exp 61 reports reproduced on the campaign core; primitives measured live and recorded as a gated apparatus record. Items 1b/3/5 are gated by T5/T6, not T1.
