## 1. Hardware safety — read before commanding motion

**Motors have been physically destroyed twice.** The two hardware-damage invariants live in CLAUDE.md **core** — read them before writing any motion code:

- **Head is WORLD-frame** (`reachy-head-world-frame`): `goto_target(body_yaw=X)` with `head=None` COUNTER-ROTATES the head — `head=None` means "re-solve IK against the RETAINED world head target", never "leave the head alone". Any body turn followed by a head-mounted-sensor read MUST ship an explicit `head=` matrix with the body delta added to head yaw.
- **Single clamped dispatch point** (`reachy-motion-dispatch-safety`): `ReachyMiniController.goto_target` is the only clamped+locked motion dispatch; `motion/movement.py::move_head` is the only other sanctioned SDK primitive. Never hand-roll `mini.goto_target(...)` / `mini.set_target(...)` / `mini.look_at_image(...)`. Callers reporting pose outcomes read `last_clamped_axes` or the frame readback, never echo the commanded value.

Standing gotchas around those rules: after any robot reflash, **version-match SDK vs daemon FIRST** (skew fails silently); **torque is a separate explicit gate** — `wake_up()` no longer enables torque, motion silently no-ops while reads work until `enable_motors()` runs (the controller's `wake_up()` does this); `goto_target(head=...)` takes a 4x4 pose matrix, not euler tuples.

**[engineering] Reachy Mini transport is WS-era (SDK >= 1.5): control `ws://<host>:8000/ws/sdk`, liveness `GET /api/daemon/status`, network DoA `GET /api/state/doa`.** Never re-introduce zenoh `:7447` probes/tunnels, multicast-discovery debugging, or client-side `mini.media.get_DoA()` for off-robot consumers. After any robot reflash, version-match client vs daemon FIRST (`curl .../api/daemon/status` vs `importlib.metadata.version("reachy-mini")`) — skew fails silently; torque is a separate explicit gate (`enable_motors()`); `goto_target(head=...)` takes a 4x4 pose matrix. Full history: [docs/lessons/reachy-ws-transport.md](docs/lessons/reachy-ws-transport.md). Regression guard: [tests/unit/test_reachy_connection_options.py](tests/unit/test_reachy_connection_options.py) (WS-era pins: probe :8000 not :7447, host/port threading, tunnel :8000 retarget, era gate fails loud) + ad-hoc `grep -rn "7447" src/maxim/` must match only historical comments.

## 2. Mental model

SEM (Sensor-Effector-Modulator) entities live in `embodiment/sem.py`: an `Entity` tree carries `Sensor`s (readings), `Modulator`s (qualified sub-sensors like `arms.thermal`), and `AffordanceSchema`s (actions with `requires` gating plus `self_effect`/`target_effect` sensor deltas). The agent's own entity is wrapped by a `Body`; **`Body.evaluate_failures()` is both the tick and the convergence point** — it applies wall-clock `tick_vital_drift` lazily on every call, then evaluates failures, so every path that touches the body (affordance execution, sim tools, the per-proposal substrate tick, the LLM-primary per-iteration tick) advances drives and can produce pain. Drives are declared per-body in YAML: **homeostatic** (drift toward `set_point`, pain beyond `comfort_band`) vs **entropic** (drift away from equilibrium, pain at `deprivation_threshold`).

Pain and credit then flow through **three channels with deliberately different semantics** — no single mechanism serves all three jobs:

| Channel | Path | Semantics | Filter / gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — direct attribution | `FailureEvent` → `ToolOutput.side_effects["embodiment_failures"]` → `ToolPainBridge.record_tool_embodiment_failure` → `nac.record_outcome` | **state-based**, one event per call while breached; direct `(tool_name, invocation_id)` key, no context similarity | **B8 delta-attribution** — a tool is blamed only for breaches its OWN effect caused (state-independent, so it works even at sensor saturation) |
| 2 — bus broadcast | `_publish_drive_pain` → PainBus → subscribers (NAc learner, hippocampus capture, Wire-2 valence, `PainCircuitBridge`) | **severity-latched on the `Entity`** — fires on band entry + material re-injury, clears with hysteresis | deepen-fraction threshold + hysteresis; unreadable sensor clears the latch |
| 3 — motor credit | `drive_potential_diff` side-effect → `record_outcome` takes its **SIGN** (±1) | **value-progress toward comfort**, not pain and not tool-execution success | **producer-owned collateral-harm gate** — suppressed to `None` when the action harmed a sensor its progress didn't account for |

Why they differ: channel 1 has a direct lookup key, so context-similarity attribution is wrong there (the fallback is only for out-of-band pain); channel 2 must not spam per-tick re-publishes but must also never let a body "go silent" about deepening injury; channel 3 must credit *direction* (turn TOWARD the sound, warm the cold body) — a ±1 tool-success signal is direction-blind and a pain-based reward misses all sub-threshold entropic relief. Latching channel 1 or moving the harm gate to the consumer each provably inverts learned behavior (see the A20/A21/A22 stubs below).

Rich pain context rides **PainBus** (`PainSignal.context`, free-form cause description); the typed, isolation-ruled surface is **ReactionBus**. Motivation is untouched by both pain channels — it rides the drive VALUE (`body_state_summary`, acting coach).

Also see [docs/agents/bio-memory.md](bio-memory.md) for the NAc consumer side (directional `_context_similarity` denominator contract, reward-bias clamp, cluster rewards) and [docs/agents/runtime-tools.md](runtime-tools.md) for `build_executor`/`build_pain_bus` wiring and the `ToolOutput.side_effects` key registry.

## 4. Invariants & lessons

**[engineering] Tool-invoked embodiment pain attributes directly, not via context similarity.** `runtime/executor.py` routes non-empty `side_effects["embodiment_failures"]` to `ToolPainBridge.record_tool_embodiment_failure(tool_name, invocation_id, failures)` → `nac.record_outcome` with a direct event_id — NO context similarity. `_on_embodiment_pain` early-returns while any tool is in flight (guard assumes one Executor + one bridge per agent; if a future refactor shares a bridge across concurrent tools, narrow the guard by matching `signal.context["entity"]`). Full history: [docs/lessons/direct-key-over-context-similarity.md](docs/lessons/direct-key-over-context-similarity.md). Regression guard: [tests/substrate/test_sem_pain_cascade.py](tests/substrate/test_sem_pain_cascade.py) (end-to-end direct-attribution path validated). *(The underlying principle — direct key beats context similarity; `_context_similarity` is directional — is homed in [docs/agents/bio-memory.md](bio-memory.md).)*

**[engineering] A tool is blamed only for drive-spec breaches its OWN effect caused (B8 delta-attribution).** `ModulatorAffordanceTool.execute` includes a drive-spec failure in `side_effects["embodiment_failures"]` only when the affordance's own delta is intrinsically harmful to that sensor; standard (non-drive) `failure_mode`s pass through unchanged; on any filter error fall back to the UNFILTERED events, never to empty. B8 is NOT redundant and must NOT be removed — being state-independent, it is the only mechanism separating causer from bystander at sensor saturation (latching this channel inverts a repeat harmful affordance to POSITIVE credit). Full history: [docs/lessons/b8-delta-attribution.md](docs/lessons/b8-delta-attribution.md). Regression guard: [tests/unit/test_substrate_primary_scene_harm.py](tests/unit/test_substrate_primary_scene_harm.py) (B8 helper unit tests + execute-level causing-vs-bystander on the chilled body) + [tests/substrate/test_sem_pain_cascade.py](tests/substrate/test_sem_pain_cascade.py) + [tests/unit/test_self_effect.py](tests/unit/test_self_effect.py) (causing-tool path preserved).

**[engineering] Drive-pain emission is CHANNEL-SPLIT: the direct `FailureEvent` channel stays state-based (one event per call while breached — do NOT latch it; latching starves B8); the PainBus channel is severity-latched** (fires on band entry + material re-injury past `_BREACH_DEEPEN_FRACTION`, clears with hysteresis). The latch lives on `Entity.drive_breach_severity` (`__slots__`, never serialized), NOT the `Embodiment` wrapper; an unreadable sensor clears the latch; motivation rides the drive VALUE and is untouched by both channels. Note opposite polarities: `FailureMode.persistent` = keep firing; `drive_breach_severity` = stay quiet unless deepening — declaring `persistent: true` on a drive does nothing. Full history: [docs/lessons/drive-pain-channel-split.md](docs/lessons/drive-pain-channel-split.md). Regression guard: [tests/unit/test_transition_drive_pain.py](tests/unit/test_transition_drive_pain.py) (19 tests — channel-1 stays state-based incl. at saturation, channel-2 entry/deepen/marginal-creep/recovery/band-edge-jitter/entropic-satisfaction-threshold, entity-owned latch across ephemeral wrappers + same-name siblings + reparent, unreadable-sensor clear, non-serialization, motivation preserved) + [tests/unit/test_substrate_primary_scene_harm.py::test_execute_delta_attribution_causing_vs_bystander_on_chilled_body](tests/unit/test_substrate_primary_scene_harm.py) (end-to-end repeat-causer arm on the real Exp 42 fixtures). *(The SCN-oscillator drive-event gap noted alongside this fold is [docs/agents/bio-memory.md](bio-memory.md) territory — see its `record_event` intake invariant.)*

**[engineering] Substrate-primary action selection is credited by the SIGN of drive VALUE-PROGRESS toward comfort (`drive_potential_diff`), not tool-execution success.** `drive_comfort_progress` is value-based, NOT pain-based (a pain-based reward books zero for sub-threshold entropic relief and starves warmth/feeding of credit); the consumer takes the SIGN (±1), never the magnitude (a graded magnitude loses the argmax to a flat +1); `target_effect` is NOT scored on the ACTOR (the one named exception: the cradle mother's feed is scored on the RECIPIENT infant and credited through the operant trace with `source="operant"` — Exp 52, `cradle_mother.reactive_mother_tick(credit="relief")`); exactly-0 net progress → tool-success fallback. The collateral-harm gate lives in the PRODUCER (suppresses to `None` on unaccounted-sensor failures); a `not embodiment_failed` gate at the consumer would break orient. Full history: [docs/lessons/motor-credit-value-progress.md](docs/lessons/motor-credit-value-progress.md). Regression guard: [tests/unit/test_drive_pain_helper.py](tests/unit/test_drive_pain_helper.py) (`drive_comfort_progress` value-based + entropic-subthreshold-positive) + [tests/unit/test_motor_credit_emission.py](tests/unit/test_motor_credit_emission.py) (emission incl. sub-threshold entropic, collateral gate on real `warmth_alpha_harm`/`_safe`, orient-preserved) + [tests/unit/test_tool_dispatch.py::TestClusterRewardMotorCredit](tests/unit/test_tool_dispatch.py) (signed consumer + producer-owns-harm-gate) + [scripts/orient_substrate/2_full_path_probe.py](scripts/orient_substrate/2_full_path_probe.py) (full-path policy) + Exp 42 behavioral re-validation.

**[engineering] PainBus (rich free-form `PainSignal.context`) and ReactionBus (typed, isolation-ruled) coexist by design.** Do NOT route rich cause-description through `ReactionContext.bindings` (violates the isolation docstring) and do NOT re-add a `ContextVar`-based signal stash as a back channel (re-entrancy hazard). PainBus's finer `(entity, failure_mode)` refractory gate is intentional — without it two entities firing embodiment pain in one tick collapse into one dispatch. Full history: [docs/lessons/painbus-rich-reactionbus-typed.md](docs/lessons/painbus-rich-reactionbus-typed.md). Regression guard: [src/maxim/proprioception/pain_bus.py](src/maxim/proprioception/pain_bus.py) (module docstring + dual-dispatch implementation) + [tests/unit/test_pain_bus.py](tests/unit/test_pain_bus.py).

**[engineering] Drive protocol: `HomeostaticDriveSpec` and `EntropicDriveSpec` are compartmentalized types** (cradle Stage 1a, 2026-04-26). Homeostatic drives (temperature, pressure, stamina) drift toward `set_point`, pain proportional to deviation beyond `comfort_band`. Entropic drives (hunger, thirst, fatigue) drift away from equilibrium, pain at `deprivation_threshold`. Both live in `embodiment/sem.py`. `CouplingSpec` and `ModulationSpec` are 1.0 interface reservations (parsed from YAML, not evaluated). `pain_model: str` field future-proofs the homeostatic pain formula. Do NOT add fields to these frozen dataclasses post-1.0 without a breaking-change plan. Regression guard: [src/maxim/embodiment/sem.py](src/maxim/embodiment/sem.py) — frozen dataclasses with `SHAPE-FROZEN at 1.0` docstring marker per CC3.

**[engineering] Entity acquisition via `entity_acquired`/`entity_released` side_effects** (cradle Stage 2, Mechanism B from proprioceptive_discovery.md). When the agent calls `pick_up` on an acquirable entity (`metadata["acquirable"] == True`), `ModulatorAffordanceTool` emits `entity_acquired` in `ToolOutput.side_effects`. `Executor._handle_entity_acquisition` reparents the entity to the agent's body, registers its tools, and flips ownership in `EntityMap`. `drop` reverses via `entity_released`. Acquired entity sensors contribute to the damage model while equipped. Regression guard: [src/maxim/embodiment/entity_map.py](src/maxim/embodiment/entity_map.py) (`transfer_to_self`/`transfer_to_scene`) + [src/maxim/runtime/executor.py::_handle_entity_acquisition](src/maxim/runtime/executor.py).

**[engineering] `self_effect` on `AffordanceSchema` writes to agent body sensors** (cradle Stage 3). Scene entity affordances declare `self_effect: {sensor: delta}` in YAML. Fires only on voluntary agent actions (not reflex/orchestrator). Supports qualified modulator sub-sensor names (`arms.thermal`). Clamped to sensor range. Logged via `sim_sensor()`. Regression guard: [src/maxim/embodiment/tool_bridge.py](src/maxim/embodiment/tool_bridge.py) (`self_effect` write-back at affordance dispatch).

**[engineering] Three interaction levels for entities:** Observe (look/examine — no contact), Touch (self_effect — brief one-time sensor delta), Pick up (entity acquisition — sustained contact, sensors join body). The three-layer sensation model: Contact (Layer 1, entity acquisition), Proximity (Layer 2, orchestrator sensor writes), Narrative (Layer 3, keyword reflex fallback). All converge on: sensor change → `evaluate_failures()` → PainBus → NAc. Regression guard: [src/maxim/embodiment/body.py::evaluate_failures](src/maxim/embodiment/body.py) — single convergence point for all three layers.

**[engineering] Writes to a LIVE-owned sensor go through `world_set_axis(..., owner=<writer>)` — an anonymous write to a sensor in `Embodiment.live_world_set_sensors` is REFUSED, not applied** (reflex-canonicalization structural half, roadmap_1_1_to_1_3.md, shipped 2026-08-13 in #508). The check lives INSIDE `embodiment/audio_localization.py::world_set_axis` (one WARNING per sensor, then silent refusal returning False), so forgetting the guard at a new caller becomes a refusal rather than a silent fabrication — a modeled/derived write to a sensor a real measurement stream owns produces a reading the next measurement reverts (the phantom-credit / `head=None` failure class). `owner=` is a SELF-DECLARATION for logs, not an ACL — do not harden it into a typed enum (that would force the embodiment layer to enumerate hardware-layer writers). Current legitimate live writers: `DoAFeed` (`owner="doa_feed"`) and the motor backend's measured readback (`owner="motor_readback"`). Sim/scripted bodies (empty live set) are unaffected; agent_loop §1.16 skips its redundant echo write when the sensor is live-owned. Regression guard: [tests/unit/test_world_set_ownership.py](tests/unit/test_world_set_ownership.py) (refusal, owner opt-out, legacy paths, warn-once) + the conftest autouse reset of the warn-dedup set.

**[engineering] The embodiment tick cycle lives in `Body.evaluate_failures()`** — it applies `tick_vital_drift` automatically from elapsed wall-clock, so drives advance on every path that touches the body. **Do NOT add a second raw `tick_vital_drift(` call site** (double-drift); adding another `evaluate_failures()` CALLER is safe (idempotent w.r.t. elapsed time). LLM-primary ticks per live loop iteration via `tick_embodiment_drift` — a no-op on substrate-primary (which ticks itself in `propose_via_substrate`) and when unembodied. `EmbodimentPerceptSource` is Dormant (CC8 protocol-shape template only). Body-state prompt wiring stays behind `MAXIM_ENABLE_BODY_STATE_PROMPT` (default OFF) pending the pre-registered ablation. Full history: [docs/lessons/embodiment-tick-cycle.md](docs/lessons/embodiment-tick-cycle.md). Regression guard: [tests/unit/test_drive_specs.py::TestEvaluateFailuresAutoDrift](tests/unit/test_drive_specs.py) (wall-clock auto-drift + no-double-drift + first-call baseline) + [tests/unit/test_substrate_primary_scene_harm.py::TestProposeViaSubstrateTick](tests/unit/test_substrate_primary_scene_harm.py) (substrate-primary tick fires before drive read) + [tests/unit/test_substrate_primary_scene_harm.py::TestTickEmbodimentDriftLLMPrimary](tests/unit/test_substrate_primary_scene_harm.py) (llm-primary per-iteration tick applies real drift; no-op on substrate-primary + unembodied; swallows exceptions) + CI grep in [.github/workflows/test.yml](.github/workflows/test.yml) restricting `tick_vital_drift(` call sites to `embodiment/body.py` + [tests/unit/test_percept_source_protocol.py::test_embodiment_percept_source_satisfies_protocol](tests/unit/test_percept_source_protocol.py) (dormant module stays protocol-conformant). *(Agent-loop tick call sites: see [docs/agents/runtime-tools.md](runtime-tools.md).)*

**[engineering] LLM-derived entity specs route through `normalize_llm_entity_spec` before `_parse_entity`** — it backstops LLM forgetfulness by filling `abstract: True` on capability-only modulators (post-C4 hard-error flip, unnormalized specs crash with `ConfigurationError`). Full history: [docs/lessons/llm-entity-spec-normalizer.md](docs/lessons/llm-entity-spec-normalizer.md). ## 5. Live gotchas / known gaps

- **Motion gotchas** (`head=None` retained-pose IK, torque gate, era-match) — see §1; they bite on every first session against a reflashed robot.
- **Two latch polarities coexist:** `FailureMode.persistent` = keep firing until recovery; `Entity.drive_breach_severity` = stay quiet unless deepening. `persistent: true` on a drive does nothing — drives are not `FailureMode`s.
- **`EmbodimentPerceptSource` is Dormant** — never production-wired; kept only as the CC8 protocol-shape template for the remote-percept adapter (mesh perception transport, re-tagged 1.2/1.3 — 1.1.0 shipped without it).
- **Exp 44 embodied llm-primary numbers still need re-validation against the Track-1 drift-tick cadence** (drift *timing* changed even though pain emission is now transition-latched). Live venue: [docs/experiments/44b_pilot.md](../experiments/44b_pilot.md) + [docs/experiments/protocols/exp44b_preregistration.md](../experiments/protocols/exp44b_preregistration.md).
- **Path trap:** the channel-split plan lives at [docs/plans/deferred/transition_based_drive_pain.md](../plans/deferred/transition_based_drive_pain.md) even though the work SHIPPED (2026-07-28) — don't read the `deferred/` label as "not built".
- **Drive `TemporalEvent`s have never reached the SCN oscillator** (unwired `distributor=` + malformed constructor swallowed by a bare except). Do NOT wire the distributor alone — both must be fixed together or neither; see [docs/agents/bio-memory.md](bio-memory.md) and [docs/plans/deferred/scn_event_producer_gap.md](../plans/deferred/scn_event_producer_gap.md).
- **Signed sensors (azimuth) FOLD without a declared range**, and the audio place-code toggle changes EC cluster identity — encoder rules and `MAXIM_PLACE_CODE_EXTEROCEPTION` are owned by [docs/agents/bio-memory.md](bio-memory.md).
- **1.0-era DoA magnitude claims were PROVISIONAL (degraded motors 2+3) — H1 re-measured on the repaired platform 2026-08-08/24:** gain 0.578 replicated, no staircase, `_big` delivered shift 0.943/0.942 (#535), the provisional flag on the `_big` magnitudes CLEARED; open hardware findings are D30 (head drift, re-measured 13–19° roll in Exp 53) and D31 (fold guard). The protocol ([docs/experiments/protocols/h1_healthy_hardware_doa_preregistration.md](../../docs/experiments/protocols/h1_healthy_hardware_doa_preregistration.md)).
