- **[engineering] Peer transports are typed per purpose, not generic** (C10 prep, 2026-06-04, transport ships in 1.1). [`_MaximPeerBackend`](src/maxim/models/language/maxim_peer_backend.py) is the LLM-inference transport — the "exactly one HTTP call, no retry, typed `BackendError`" rule is its load-bearing invariant earned via the 2026-04-12 fail-slow incident. Future peer transports (perception in 1.1 per [docs/plans/deferred/mesh_perception_transport.md](docs/plans/deferred/mesh_perception_transport.md), substrate bundle in 1.1+ per [docs/plans/maxim_hivemind.md](docs/plans/maxim_hivemind.md), any further sibling) live in their own file **next to their domain** — LLM at `src/maxim/models/language/maxim_peer_backend.py`, substrate-bundle transport will land next to [`src/maxim/hivemind/bundle.py`](src/maxim/hivemind/bundle.py) (e.g. `hivemind/peer_transport.py`), perception transport will land next to the percept surface (e.g. `mesh/perception_transport.py` or `agents/remote_percept_source.py`'s sibling). The path-by-domain placement keeps each transport co-located with the surface it serves; do NOT force a `models/<modality>/` convention that fights existing module layout. What every transport DOES share is the playbook: single-purpose backend, typed exception hierarchy with `.fix_hint`, no internal retry, `for_url(api_key=k)` factory pattern with instance-level `_api_key_override` (no `os.environ` mutation), `health_check()` as the canonical probe entry point. Do NOT extend `_MaximPeerBackend` to carry non-LLM payloads — the failover semantics differ (router-handles-retry is LLM-specific; a dropped perception frame is not a router-failover event, it's a missed observation that the agent loop tolerates by reading the next one) and the load-bearing one-call invariant becomes ambiguous when stretched across purposes. The Hivemind substrate-bundle exchange is the second-consumer test: it lands as a sibling file, not as an extension of either existing transport, so the typed-transport-per-purpose pattern is proven by parallel use. Regression guard: the existing CI grep on `maxim_peer_backend.py` (enforcing the one-HTTP-call rule via `grep -nE "retry|backoff|gateway"`) is the template the next transport copies; any new transport class adds its own CI grep allow-list entry on the same pattern so cross-transport leakage stays out.
