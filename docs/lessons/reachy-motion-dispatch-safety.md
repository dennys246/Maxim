- **[engineering] `ReachyMiniController.goto_target` is the single clamped+locked motion dispatch point; `motion/movement.py::move_head` is the only other sanctioned SDK motion primitive and composes the WORLD head pose from a BODY-RELATIVE yaw at dispatch time** (2026-08-07 safety fold; motors 2+3 were destroyed by a pose commanded beyond physical capability — roadmap_1_1_to_1_3.md hardware note). The controller clamps physical CAPABILITY limits (roll/pitch ±40°, head yaw **body-relative** ±65°, body yaw ±160°) and holds `_motion_lock` (RLock) across `get_current_pose()` → head-matrix composition → SDK dispatch (TOCTOU on live kinematic state) — `look_at_pixel` takes the same lock. The head-yaw clamp is applied in the BODY-RELATIVE frame; clamping the world-frame sum is wrong in both directions (spuriously limits a legal pose on a turned body, misses an illegal one when offsets cancel). `move_head` mirrors the same rotation clamps AND adds the current body yaw to its relative-yaw argument — the Selfy layer's frame contract is body-relative (`sync_head_position`: `yaw = world − body`) while the SDK matrix is world, and pre-fold `move_head` shipped relative AS world, so after any SEM body turn a "centered" gaze command demanded a head-relative angle up to ±160° against the ±65° neck (the destruction mechanism, reincarnated one layer up; `Selfy.turn_around`'s raw-SDK STEP 2 was the original). **Do NOT hand-roll `mini.goto_target(...)` / `mini.set_target(...)` / `mini.look_at_image(...)` anywhere else** — route through the controller or `Selfy.move()`'s clamped enqueue. A clamped dispatch logs a WARNING, records the axes on `controller.last_clamped_axes`, and still returns `True` — callers that report pose outcomes to the LLM or to learners MUST read `last_clamped_axes` or the frame back rather than echoing the commanded value (the "accepted dispatch is a promise, not a motion" class PR #459 fixed). The 65° figure is the vendor-doc capability ceiling; Exp 49's measured ~±22° came from the DEGRADED platform — H1 re-measures on healthy hardware (docs/experiments/protocols/h1_healthy_hardware_doa_preregistration.md). `SimulatedController` defaults its limits to the real controller's constants for sim-hardware parity (explicit tighter limits still win). **Retained axes are COMMANDED, not read back (F1 fold, 2026-08-09; H1 campaign):** `goto_target` fills UNSPECIFIED axes from the controller's per-axis `_last_commanded` stash (post-clamp, written only after successful dispatch), never from the live pose readback — readback fill-in is positive feedback under any achieved-vs-commanded bias (~20 yaw-only probe commands ratcheted roll to +38° during H1, forcing retraction of the first neck-envelope numbers). The readback SEEDS an axis exactly once (never-commanded, or after the stash was cleared); refuse-to-guess covers BOTH the yaw seed and the body seed when the pose is unreadable. Any motion the stash does not see must invalidate it: `look_at_pixel`/`wake_up`/`goto_sleep`/`disconnect` clear internally (clear-before-motion — a spurious clear costs one seed, a missed clear leaves stale re-commands), and the raw sanctioned movers (`move_head`, Selfy's raw `look_at_image` enqueue) call `controller.note_external_head_motion()` — a new raw head mover MUST wire the same hook or the next controller command snaps the head back to a stale pose. Under `automatic_body_yaw` the body axis is never stashed (the daemon moves it behind the runtime). A rate-limited WARNING fires when achieved diverges from last-commanded past 5° — the progressive-degradation early-warning channel the stash would otherwise silence.
