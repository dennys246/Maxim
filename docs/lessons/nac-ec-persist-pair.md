- **[engineering] NAc and EC persist as a PAIR in `build_bio_stack` (`nac.json` + `ec.json`); decay-on-load lives in `NAc.load()` and NEVER in `load_state()`** (nac_cross_session_persistence.md, 2026-07-30). NAc `reward_bias` / `cluster_reward_bias` key on EC node ids — restoring either without the other leaves biases silently dangling on nodes a fresh EC never re-allocates (persistence that looks like it works). `build_bio_stack` restores hippocampus + NAc + EC when `persistence_dir` has prior files; `MemoryHub.on_session_end` (+ `_lightweight`) saves EC beside NAc. `NAc.save()` — not `dump()` — stamps `saved_at` (`dump()` stays the pure BioSystemSnapshot surface, so hivemind bundles carry no timestamp); `NAc.load()` applies elapsed-wall-clock decay from it (`cluster_reward_bias` 1-day half-life — working signal; `reward_bias`/`goal_reward_bias`/`percept_valences` 7-day — transfer surfaces; links + Welford never decay on load). **`apply_decay=False` is REQUIRED at call sites where wall-clock gaps are not agent-experienced time**: the sim `--resume-sim` restore (sims are tick-anchored; the Exp 44 tau-hold harness depends on verbatim resume) and read-only observers (`maxim.load.nac`, `maxim.observe` — disk truth, and load→save round-trips must not compound decay). `load_state` stays byte-faithful for hivemind `nac_merge`. The sim orchestrator NPC passes `AgentConfig(load_persisted=False)` — write-but-don't-read, so `~/.maxim/orchestrator`'s unaudited accumulation can't become a cross-run narrator confound. This wall-clock forgetting is an `[engineering]`-tier calibration, NOT a validated bio claim (extinction requires unreinforced re-exposure); the 7-day `percept_valences` cell (harm-avoidance) is the flagged weakest choice. Regression guard: [tests/unit/test_nac_persistence_decay.py](tests/unit/test_nac_persistence_decay.py) (decay schedules, opt-out, bool-`saved_at`, corrupt-value coercion, recovery reset) + [tests/integration/test_cross_session_persistence.py](tests/integration/test_cross_session_persistence.py) (two-session two-process content round-trip; verified to fail on both no-persistence and a simulated save-only truncating implementation).
